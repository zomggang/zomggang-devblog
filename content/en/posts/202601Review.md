I primarily use Claude nowadays,
and I've spent considerable time on **Confluence documentation organization** for onboarding.  
Particularly, I've been creating templates and organizing backoffice-related documentation.

Working with AI to organize documents,

"Wow, the world has really gotten better."

## Reflections and Considerations 🤔

### Thoughts on My Role

The current work style is closer to Waterfall than Scrum,
with tasks structured around a ticket-based system.

Because of this,  
there were moments where I questioned:
"Where exactly is this work being used?"  
"What is my current role supposed to be?"

Although I'm positioned as a backend engineer,  
currently much of my work is more operational than development-focused.  
With major team changes expected this year,  
I want to contribute more within that flow  
and develop a clearer picture of my role.

**Key Learning**  
I realized that I need to reduce the time to deliver output.  
Rather than delaying submission while wondering "Is this right?",  
quickly sharing work and receiving feedback for improvements  
ends up being faster overall – something I want to keep in mind.

### Challenges in Creating Deliverables

I have many ideas I want to pursue,
but turning them into something I can present as "this is the result"  
is far more difficult than I expected.

I also realized that creating something by myself  
is easier than creating something that teammates can actually use  
and will continue to utilize in the future.

### Challenges with AI Utilization 🤖

AI is convenient and fast when used individually,
but I feel I still lack the ability to expand the prompts and know-how I've created  
into a form that the entire team can easily use.  
This is something I want to study more in the future.  
(I believe we'll eventually find answers together as a team.)

I also continue to consider  
**how much quality from AI-generated output we should accept**.
